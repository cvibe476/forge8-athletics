CrossFit Forge8 is a gym for anyone who wants to get stronger, move better, and feel good doing it. Whether you've never touched a barbell or you've been training for years, our coaches meet you exactly where you are.

Every class is coached, every workout is scalable, and every person who walks through the door is treated like they belong here. Because they do.

Training for Hyrox? Our Thursday classes blend CrossFit and Hyrox-specific work - making CrossFit Forge8 one of the few gyms in San Diego where you can train seriously for both.
