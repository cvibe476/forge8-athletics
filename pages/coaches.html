Angeline "Gigi" Redman
CF-L2 USAW-L1 BS Business Management

I didn't grow up as a competitive athlete and was often too shy to participate in team sports. Instead, I gravitated toward individual activities like running, yoga, and strength training. Everything changed when a friend introduced me to CrossFit. From my first class, I was hooked. What started as a workout quickly became a lifestyle. I was drawn to the challenge, the community, and especially the technical side of weightlifting. CrossFit not only transformed my physical fitness but also helped me build confidence, resilience, and mental toughness that carried over into every aspect of my life.

My passion for coaching began when I started leading small-group workouts for coworkers and friends. Watching people gain confidence, improve their fitness, and accomplish goals they once thought were out of reach sparked something in me. I realized that helping others succeed was just as rewarding as achieving my own fitness milestones. I have been coaching CrossFit since 2022 and earned my USA Weightlifting Level 1 certification to further develop my knowledge of movement, strength, and Olympic lifting. These experiences have strengthened my ability to coach athletes of all skill levels and help them move more efficiently and confidently.

I believe every athlete is unique and deserves coaching that meets them where they are. Building trust through communication, encouragement, and consistency is at the heart of my approach. I strive to create an environment where athletes feel supported, challenged, and empowered to grow. I emphasize quality over quantity, prioritizing sound movement mechanics and proper technique before adding intensity or load. Whether your goal is to improve performance, build strength, or simply become a healthier version of yourself, I'm committed to helping you reach your goals safely and confidently. There's nothing more rewarding than seeing athletes discover their potential and achieve things they never thought possible. Your success is my success, and I'm honored to be part of that journey.

Coach & Owner
Jeong Kim
CF-L1 BS Electrical Engineering BS Biomedical Engineering Duke University

I have always wanted to stay active and healthy and keep up with daughters who play multiple sports in the club, but having a work and life style that I could not really fit a workout into, it had not been happening for a long time. Reuniting with my best friend Jesse, who happens to be one of the best CrossFit coaches and owners around, got me motivated to start the gym and training. I am in the best condition of my life since college. Now all my bloodwork comes out fine and I can chase after my dog who always runs away from us. Ask me for the proof and I will happily provide.

When I turned 40, a few things happened. Bloodwork started to go bad and I could not keep up with two teenage daughters who play sports at an elite level. Visits to 24 Hour Fitness often ended up becoming binge-watching Netflix opportunities. While I walked miles irregularly, it was not helping me stay fit and active. My first CrossFit class was different. It was a team effort, all the other members and the coach were there to support, and I wanted to push my limit. Simply put, I just had to show up to see the difference, and it only took one month.

A CrossFit gym has members from all different backgrounds, yet it is about competing against yourself, not other members. Ego should not play a role in pushing yourself beyond your capability. My goal is making sure members understand their limitations and capability, enjoy subtle yet clear improvements every time they come in, and that safety always comes first. I love seeing members make consistent progress from taking our classes.

How We Coach

THE STANDARD
WE HOLD.

Technique First

We never rush movement quality to chase intensity. Every athlete - beginner or advanced - earns the right to load by demonstrating sound mechanics first.

Intentional Scaling

Scaling isn't a crutch - it's how training stays safe and effective for every body. Our coaches scale with purpose, not just convenience.

No Athlete Left Behind

The fastest athlete finishes first. The last athlete finishes loudest. We cheer for every rep in the room, every time.

Continuous Development

Our coaches are constantly learning - attending clinics, earning certifications, and refining their craft. You deserve the best-trained eye in the room.
