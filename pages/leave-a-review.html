A REVIEW.

If you have trained at Forge8, a few honest lines on Google is the single most useful thing you can do for the gym. It takes about thirty seconds and it is what people in Miramar see before they walk in.

Scan With Your Phone

Point your camera at the code. It opens the Google review box for Forge8 directly — no searching, no scrolling past other gyms.

  1. 01
    Tap the button below
    It opens straight to the review form for CrossFit Forge8 Athletics.
  2. 02
    Pick your stars
    Rate what your experience was actually like.
  3. 03
    Say what stood out
    One or two sentences is plenty. The coaching, the 5 AM class, the 24-hour open gym, the people — whatever was true for you.
Write a Review maps.google.com/?cid=6062255210617642062

Only if you have actually trained here. Reviews from people who have not been to the gym do not help anyone, and Google removes them when it notices.

We do not edit or filter what you write. If something was not right, say that too — a gym that only has five-star reviews reads as fake, and a real note about what to expect is worth more to the next person than praise is.
