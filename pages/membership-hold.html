Hold Policy: If you need to temporarily pause your membership, complete this form to submit your hold request. Holds are available for a minimum of 2 weeks and a maximum of 3 months per calendar year. Your membership billing will pause on your requested hold start date and resume automatically on your hold end date. Requests must be submitted at least 3 days before the requested start date. If you are looking to cancel your membership permanently, submit a cancellation request instead.
