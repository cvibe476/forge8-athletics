Common Questions

GOT QUESTIONS?

Do I need experience to join?
Not at all. Every workout is scalable and our coaches will meet you exactly where you are. We have athletes of all experience levels training side-by-side every day.
What should I bring to my first class?
Just athletic clothes and a pair of flat-soled shoes (cross-trainers or lifters are ideal, but any sneakers work). We'll take care of the rest. Show up 10 minutes early so we can get you set up.
Is there a contract or commitment?
No long-term contracts. Monthly memberships are billed month-to-month and can be cancelled anytime with 30 days notice.
What's included in the free trial?
The free trial gives you 3 classes to use within 7 days, at no cost. Classes are coach-led and open to all levels. Open gym access is not included. Sign up to get started.
Can I try a class before committing?
Yes - drop-ins are always available. Come try a class for $30 and see if CrossFit Forge8 Athletics is the right fit for you.
What's included in Open Gym?
Unlimited members get 24-hour open gym access - full facility, all equipment, any time of day or night. A signed waiver is required, which you'll complete during the PushPress sign-up flow.
Is the gym safe to use after hours alone?
Yes - the facility is secured, well-lit, and camera-monitored 24/7. We ask that members train responsibly after hours: use appropriate weights, don't train movements to failure without a spotter, and always have your phone with you.
